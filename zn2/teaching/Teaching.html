Teaching Philosophy

Physics that clicks

Every course combines rigorous theory with intuition-building interaction questions, worked "scribbles", recorded sessions and Mathematica visualizations — so students see the physics, not just the equations.

  • Interactive questions & brain-teasers in every course
  • Computational physics with Mathematica codes
  • Distance-learning short-courses through the Saudi Physical Society
  • Content available in both English and Arabic
