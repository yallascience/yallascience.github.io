Academic Journey

From glowing silicon to clean energy

During his doctoral studies at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, Dr. Yamani worked on silicon nanocrystallites — tiny particles that glow brilliantly under UV light. Several published papers and two patents came out of this research, for which he was awarded the King Abdul-Aziz Medal of Honor.

In Dhul-Hijjah 1428 H he was appointed Founding Director of the Center of Excellence in Nanotechnology (CENT), building KFUPM's nanomaterials program for petroleum and petrochemical applications.

Today his group fuses experiment, DFT simulation and machine learning to tackle hydrogen production, separation and storage, carbon management and next-generation batteries.

  • Public lectures on nanotechnology & CENT
  • Board member, Saudi Physical Society (SPS) & Director of its Teachers' Division
  • Passionate about popularizing science in Arabic
