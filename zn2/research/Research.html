Research

Six focus areas

Interconnected pillars driving breakthrough discoveries at the frontier of clean-energy science.

01

Hydrogen Production & Storage

Steam methane reforming (SMR), water-gas shift (WGS), and advanced storage systems including oil-shale hydrogen extraction for domestic scalability.

02

Pd Membrane Separation

Palladium-based dense membranes for ultra-pure H₂ separation integrated with reforming reactors, plus YSZ-protected Pd membrane systems.

03

Electrocatalysis (OER & HER)

Advanced electrocatalysts — FeP, NiP, NiFeP, Co/Mn-oxide systems — for oxygen and hydrogen evolution in alkaline and acidic media.

Lab Capabilities

What we can do

State-of-the-art experimental infrastructure coupled with cutting-edge computational tools.

Synthesis

Hydrothermal, sol–gel, impregnation, thin-film, solid state, molten salt, electrodeposition, probe sonication, co-precipitation.

Electrochemistry

LSV, CV, EIS, GCD, chronoamperometry; full battery fabrication and testing.

Gas & Reactors

High P–T batch reactors, in-situ gas injection (H₂, CO₂, CH₄), reforming & gasification studies.

Characterization

XRD, SEM, TEM, XPS, BET, XRF, EDS, FTIR, Raman, TOF-SIMS, TOC, Rock-Eval, GC, MS.

DFT Simulations

Quantum Espresso and Open-MX for first-principles electronic structure.

Multiphysics

COMSOL for coupled transport, reactor simulations and thermal modeling.

Machine Learning

MATLAB regression and scikit-learn pipelines for catalyst screening.

Data Visualization

Python, LaTeX, Origin and Excel for publication-quality figures.
